# RankCard

The rank as a field guide's title page: "being the field notes of an Elite Elephant Wrangler", rank and count, distance to the next rank.

**Maps to:** `HomeView` `SillyTitleBanner` and `SillyTitleEngine` tiers (0 Unranked Future Legend, 1 Starter Rookie Reps, 2 Iron Aspiring Animal Accumulator, 4 Bronze Certified Hippo Hoister, 8 Silver Elite Elephant Wrangler, 12 Gold Legendary Giraffe Stacker, 16 Platinum Mythical Whale Chaser, 20 Diamond Grand Poobah of Perpetual Motion).

**Consumer provides:** title, rank name, unlocked count, total (42), next rank + threshold. Optional: the three-row neighbourhood list.

**Do**
- Centre the page on `surface` inside a double hairline frame (border + inset outline, both `line`).
- Title in `display`; rank line in `label` + `accent-2`; "4 badges to Gold" in `body`.
- Pick "a" or "an" from the title's first letter.

**Don't**
- Don't use medal emoji or metallic colours for ranks. The rank word does the work.

**Tokens:** `surface`, `line`, `ink`, `ink-muted`, `accent`, `accent-2`, `display`, `caption`, `label`.
