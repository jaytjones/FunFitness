The monthly challenge panel with emoji, title, blurb and segmented progress.

**Maps to:** HomeView `ChallengeCard`.

**Consumer provides**
- `challenge` (emoji, title, blurb, target), `done` and `target` in display units, `fraction`.

**Do**
- Use the challenge's own copy: "Rack up a spooky 66.6 km before the pumpkins rot."
- Readout in the user's units: 3.4 / 41.4 mi · 8%.

**Don't**
- Don't recolour per season beyond the emoji; the type colour carries it.

**Tokens:** `surface`, `type-distance`, `ink`, `ink-muted`, `progress-track`, `headline`, `hud`.
