## The short version

FunFitness doesn't collect your personal data. We don't run servers that receive your information, we don't
use analytics or advertising, and we don't track you. Your workouts stay on your devices, in Apple Health if
you choose, and in your own iCloud account if you turn on sync. We can't see any of it.

## What the app stores, and where

| Information | Where it's kept | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Your workout log: distances, weights, reps, times, notes, exercises, streaks, badges, challenges | On your device, and in your private iCloud account if iCloud Sync is on | Only you |
| Routes of walks and runs you track | Same as above, and in Apple Health if you save workouts there | Only you |
| Profile details you enter: name, email, birthday, height, weight, goal, photo | On your device, and in your private iCloud account if iCloud Sync is on | Only you |
| Settings (units, reminders, comparison pack) | On your device / your iCloud | Only you |

Nothing in this table is sent to us. iCloud data is stored by Apple in your account under Apple's privacy
policy; we don't have access to it.

## Apple Health

Using Apple Health is optional, and you choose what FunFitness may read or write in the Health permission
screen. You can change this any time in the Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › FunFitness.

- **Read:** walking, running, hiking and cycling workouts (so they count toward your totals), heart rate
  recorded during walks and runs you track in FunFitness, and your body weight (to estimate calories for
  strength sessions).
- **Write:** walks, runs and strength sessions you log or track in FunFitness, with routes and estimated
  calories, so your workouts live in one place.

Health information is used only to show you your own progress. We never sell it, share it with anyone, use it
for advertising or marketing, or use it to build profiles. [If heart rate stays on the device per the 1a
decision: "Heart rate read from Health stays on your device."]

## Location and motion

- **Location** is used only while you track a walk or run, to measure distance and draw your route. FunFitness
  doesn't use your location at any other time.
- **Motion and fitness** (step count) is used while you track an indoor walk or run, to estimate distance.

## Apple Watch, widgets and Siri

The Apple Watch app, Home Screen widgets, Live Activities and Siri/Shortcuts work with the same information on
your devices. Information passes directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch; it isn't sent to us.

## Notifications

Reminders (streak at risk, milestone nudges, weekly recap, comparison of the day) are optional, scheduled on
your device, and can be turned off in the app or in Settings.

## Exports

If you export your log (CSV or JSON), the file goes wherever you choose to send it. That's under your control.

## What we receive

- **Nothing from the app itself.** There are no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
- **Apple may share with us** crash reports and usage statistics from people who opted in to share them with
  developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), and TestFlight feedback from beta
  testers. These are anonymous or provided by you, and we use them only to fix problems.
- **If you email us,** we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply.

## Children

FunFitness is rated 4+ and doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

- Delete individual workouts in History, or use Profile › Clear All Data.
- Deleting the app removes its data from the device. If iCloud Sync was on, remove the app's iCloud data in
  Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › FunFitness.
- Workouts saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date. If FunFitness adds features that
