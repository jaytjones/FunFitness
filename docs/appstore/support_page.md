## Common questions

**How do I log a workout?**
Tap the **+** button. Pick distance, weight, time or reps, enter the amount and save. Press and hold **+** to
choose a type directly or repeat your last entry.

**How do I track a walk or run?**
Tap **+** › **Track a Walk or Run**, choose **Outdoor** (GPS and a route map) or **Indoor** (distance from your
steps, for treadmills), then Start. Your workout shows on the Lock Screen while it runs.

**Why don't my workouts show in the Apple Fitness app?**
Turn on **Profile › Save workouts to Apple Health**, and allow FunFitness to write workouts in Settings ›
Health › Data Access & Devices › FunFitness. Very short sessions (under a minute) aren't saved.

**My workouts from Apple Health aren't counting.**
Allow FunFitness to read workouts in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › FunFitness, then open
FunFitness. Walking, running, hiking and cycling workouts are imported.

**How do I sync between my iPhone and iPad / another iPhone?**
Sign in to the same iCloud account on both, and turn on **Profile › iCloud Sync**. It can take a few minutes.

**Can I switch between miles and kilometers?**
Yes: Profile › Units.

**What are Effort Points?**
One weekly score for any kind of movement: minutes moved, weighted by how hard you worked (heart rate when
it's available). Aim for 150 points a week.

**How do streaks and shields work?**
Log at least one workout each week to keep your streak. A streak shield covers one missed week each month.

**How do I export my data?**
Profile › **Export Data (CSV)**, then share or save the file.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete workouts in History, or use Profile › Clear All Data. See the privacy policy for removing iCloud data.
