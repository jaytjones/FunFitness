# EmptyState

A friendly card for screens with nothing to show yet: a stacked-shapes illustration, a short title, one sentence, one button.

**Maps to:** Achievements "No Achievements Yet", Recent Achievements on Home, Progress with no logs, History with no entries.

**Rules**
- Title is short and playful; the sentence says exactly what unlocks the screen. Reuse the app's lines: "Log your first activity to unlock achievements!"
- One button that does the thing (opens the log sheet), never "Learn more".
- No sad faces, no grey. The illustration uses the normal palette.

**Tokens:** `surface`, illustration classes, `title` at 24pt, `body`, `ink-muted`, primary Button.
